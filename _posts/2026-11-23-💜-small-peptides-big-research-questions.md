🧬 **Small molecules. Plenty to explore.**

Peptides are short chains of amino acids—the building blocks of proteins. Some act as messengers in the body, helping cells communicate about appetite, growth, and other important functions.

🔬 **Different peptides, different research interests**

* **MOTS-c:** Studied for its role in cellular fuel use and metabolic signaling.
* **DSIP:** Explored in small human studies involving sleep, with some reporting improvements in falling asleep and sleep efficiency.
* **BPC-157:** Investigated in laboratory and animal research involving tissue repair.
* **IGF-1 LR3:** Used in laboratory research to explore growth signaling and muscle-cell development.

💜 **Why we love the science**

Each peptide offers a different way to investigate how cells work. The exciting part is learning what those signals might tell us—with findings interpreted in the context of the studies behind them.
