🧬 **WHAT IS TESAMORELIN?**

Tesamorelin is a **growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analogue**.

Researchers study how GHRH signaling stimulates the pituitary gland, which can increase endogenous growth-hormone secretion and downstream IGF-1 signaling.

Tesamorelin also has an established human clinical history. FDA-approved tesamorelin products exist for a specific indication involving excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV and lipodystrophy.

That specific approval should not be generalized to ordinary weight loss, bodybuilding, anti-aging, or unapproved research products.

**In simple terms:** tesamorelin works upstream in the growth-hormone pathway by acting through GHRH signaling.
