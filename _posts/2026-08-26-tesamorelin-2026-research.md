🧬 **TESAMORELIN: POPULATION MATTERS**

A research headline can sound broad until you look at **who was actually studied**.

2026 meta-analyses examined randomized trials of tesamorelin in people living with HIV who had HIV-associated lipodystrophy. Researchers evaluated outcomes including visceral adipose tissue, body composition, hepatic fat, metabolic measures, and safety.

That's meaningful human research, but it shouldn't be casually generalized into a claim about weight loss for everyone.

The population, indication, study design, and endpoints all matter.

That's why we always read past the headline. 🧬
