🧬 **WHAT IS 5-AMINO-1MQ?**

5-Amino-1MQ is a little different from many of the other topics in this library:

**It is a small molecule, not a peptide.**

Research interest centers on inhibition of an enzyme called **nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT)**.

NNMT participates in nicotinamide metabolism and methyl-group chemistry, which has made it a target of experimental metabolic research.

5-Amino-1MQ has been investigated primarily in **preclinical** models. That means findings from cells or animals should not be treated as established therapeutic effects in humans.

**In simple terms:** it is an experimental NNMT inhibitor used to investigate a metabolic pathway — not another GLP-1 or growth-hormone peptide.
