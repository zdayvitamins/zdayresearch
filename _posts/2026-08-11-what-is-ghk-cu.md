🧬 **WHAT IS GHK-CU?**

GHK-Cu is a **copper-binding tripeptide** — three amino acids associated with a copper ion.

It occurs naturally in the body and has been studied for decades.

Research has explored GHK-Cu in areas involving extracellular-matrix biology, collagen-related processes, fibroblasts, skin biology, angiogenesis, and tissue remodeling.

A lot of the interesting mechanistic work is laboratory or preclinical research, so the strength of evidence depends on the specific claim being discussed.

**In simple terms:** it is a tiny copper-carrying peptide with a surprisingly long research history.
