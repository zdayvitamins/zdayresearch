🧬 What Is KLOW?

KLOW combines four research peptides that are being studied in different areas of tissue and inflammation research:

• GHK-Cu — studied for collagen production, skin repair and tissue remodeling.

• BPC-157 — studied mainly in animal and laboratory research for tissue healing, including muscles, tendons and the digestive system.

• TB-500 — studied for processes involved in deeper tissue repair, cell movement, blood-vessel formation and recovery from tissue injury.

• KPV — studied for its potential anti-inflammatory effects, including research involving the gut and skin.

✨ The “Pretty Peptide” Blend

KLOW is sometimes nicknamed the “pretty peptide” blend in peptide communities, largely because GHK-Cu is associated with skin and collagen research while the other components are studied across repair and inflammation pathways.
