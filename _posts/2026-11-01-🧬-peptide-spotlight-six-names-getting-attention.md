🧬 **What’s behind the peptide buzz?**

BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, MOTS-c, Semax, and Epitalon have been getting attention—but each has a different research focus. Here’s the simple version:

🩹 **BPC-157 — Tissue repair & gut research**
Often discussed for injury recovery, with research exploring tissue repair and intestinal inflammation. Strong human evidence for healing injuries remains limited.

💪 **TB-500 — Wound-healing research**
Studied for processes involved in cell movement and tissue repair. Its recovery reputation goes beyond what has been established in human studies.

🔥 **KPV — Inflammation research**
A short peptide being explored for its potential to calm inflammatory signals, with interest in inflammatory conditions and wound healing.

⚡ **MOTS-c — Metabolism research**
A mitochondria-derived peptide attracting interest for how cells use energy, including research related to metabolic health, obesity, and bone health.

🧠 **Semax — Brain & nerve research**
Getting attention for possible effects on brain and nerve function. Research and regulatory discussions have included reduced blood flow to the brain, migraines, and certain types of nerve pain.

🌙 **Epitalon — Sleep & aging research**
Frequently discussed in longevity circles, with research interest in sleep and biological aging. Claims that it extends human lifespan are not established.

🔬 **Interesting possibilities, with more to learn**

These are research interests—not guaranteed benefits. **The strength of human evidence varies, and important questions about safety and effectiveness remain.**
