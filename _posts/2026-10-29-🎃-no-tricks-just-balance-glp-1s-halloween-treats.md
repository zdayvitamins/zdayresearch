🍬 **A little candy doesn’t have to be scary!**

Being on a wellness journey doesn’t mean missing out on Halloween. **You can work toward your goals and still enjoy an occasional treat.** Think balance—not perfection!

🧬 **Where do GLP-1s fit in?**

GLP-1 is a peptide hormone involved in appetite and fullness. Research on products that act on this signal shows how they can help people feel satisfied with less food. That doesn’t mean every sweet has to disappear from the menu!

🎃 **Enjoy your favorites—without the guilt**

Pick a small portion of the candy you actually love, eat slowly, and enjoy it. **An occasional treat can fit into a balanced routine.** There’s no need to turn a fun holiday into a test of willpower.

👻 **Listen to your stomach**

Research GLP1s can cause digestive symptoms. Smaller portions, eating slowly, and stopping when comfortably full may help. **Rich, high-fat treats like chocolate may be harder to tolerate for some people**, so pay attention to how you feel.

💧 **Keep the basics in the mix**

Stay hydrated and prioritize balanced, protein-rich meals alongside the treats. Enjoy the holiday, then continue your usual routine.

🔦 **Keep the spooky fun safe**

Bring a flashlight or glow sticks, stay together, and watch for cars while trick-or-treating. Drivers—slow down and look out for little ghosts and goblins!

💜 **Make memories, enjoy a treat, and give yourself some grace. Your wellness journey can include celebrations, too.**

**Happy Halloween from Z Day Research!** 🎃💛
