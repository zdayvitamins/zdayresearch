🧬 **RETATRUTIDE PHASE 3: TRIUMPH-1**

TRIUMPH-1 moved retatrutide research into a large Phase 3 obesity trial.

In reported results, participants receiving the 12 mg dose had an average **28.3% reduction in body weight at 80 weeks**.

That's a trial average — not a promise of what any individual would experience.

The study gives researchers a much larger human dataset than the earlier Phase 2 work.

Retatrutide remains investigational and is not FDA-approved as of this post.

Big number, but context still matters. 🧬
