🧬 **NOT ALL RESEARCH MEANS THE SAME THING**

You'll notice that our articles use labels like **Human Research, Animal Research, Cell Research, Preclinical Research,** and **General Education**.

A result in cultured cells isn't automatically a result in a mouse — and a result in a mouse isn't automatically a result in a person.

**Cell research** can help study mechanisms in a controlled environment.

**Animal research** can answer questions involving a whole living system, but species differences matter.

**Human research** gets closer to the questions people usually care about, but study design, sample size, population, endpoints, and controls still matter.

The evidence label isn't there to make a post sound fancy. It's there to help you understand **what the research actually supports**.

Stay curious, research friends. 🧬
