🧬 **WHAT IS SERMORELIN?**

Sermorelin is related to **growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH)** — one of the signals the body uses to communicate with the pituitary gland.

Rather than being growth hormone itself, the research interest is in the signaling pathway that can stimulate the pituitary to release growth hormone.

That makes sermorelin useful for understanding the **GHRH → pituitary → growth hormone → IGF-1** pathway.

Researchers are interested in this system because natural growth-hormone secretion is pulsatile rather than constant.

**In simple terms:** sermorelin research is about signaling the pituitary, not simply supplying growth hormone directly.
