🧬 **RETATRUTIDE: WHY THREE RECEPTORS?**

Retatrutide is being studied as a **triple hormone receptor agonist**.

One molecule is designed to activate receptors for **GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon**.

Researchers are studying whether combining those three signaling pathways changes outcomes involving body weight and cardiometabolic biology compared with targeting fewer pathways.

That's what makes retatrutide scientifically interesting: not simply “another GLP-1,” but a molecule designed around three receptor systems.

As of this post, retatrutide remains **investigational and is not FDA-approved**.

Research first. Hype second. 🧬
