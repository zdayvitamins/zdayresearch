🧬 **WHAT IS PT-141?**

PT-141 is also known as **bremelanotide**.

It is a melanocortin receptor agonist studied for signaling in the central nervous system and for effects involving **sexual desire and sexual function**.

That is different from medicines whose primary mechanism is vascular. Bremelanotide research instead centers on melanocortin signaling in the brain.

A regulated prescription version, **Vyleesi**, is FDA-approved for a specific population of premenopausal women with acquired, generalized hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD). Its label specifically does not establish it as a general sexual-performance enhancer or an indication for men.

That approval applies to the regulated prescription medicine and labeled indication, not unapproved research products.
