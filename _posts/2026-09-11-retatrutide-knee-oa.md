🧬 **WHY STUDY WEIGHT AND KNEE PAIN TOGETHER?**

One branch of the retatrutide Phase 3 program examined adults with **obesity or overweight and knee osteoarthritis**.

Researchers evaluated more than body weight. They also measured knee-osteoarthritis pain and related outcomes.

Reported Phase 3 results showed substantial average weight reduction alongside improvement in measured knee-pain scores.

That doesn't mean retatrutide should simply be described as a knee-pain treatment. The study population had obesity/overweight plus knee osteoarthritis, and the relationship between weight change, metabolic effects, and pain outcomes deserves careful interpretation.

Retatrutide remains investigational and is not FDA-approved as of this post. 🧬
