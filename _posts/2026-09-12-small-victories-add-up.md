💜 **SMALL VICTORIES ADD UP**

Not every bit of progress has to be dramatic.

Sometimes it's making the better choice a little more often. Getting your protein in. Moving more. Sleeping better. Learning something new. Staying consistent when motivation isn't perfect.

Tools can be useful, but they don't replace the boring stuff that actually builds consistency.

A good week doesn't have to be perfect to count.

Keep stacking the small victories, research friends. 🧬
