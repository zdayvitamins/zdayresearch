🧬 **WHAT IS TIRZEPATIDE?**

Tirzepatide is a **dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist**.

Those are two signaling systems involved in metabolic regulation. Researchers have studied tirzepatide extensively for effects involving glucose regulation, appetite and food intake, body weight, and other metabolic outcomes.

**Why is the “dual” part interesting?**

Instead of acting through only GLP-1 signaling, tirzepatide activates both **GIP + GLP-1 receptors**. That gives researchers a way to study how the two incretin pathways work together.

FDA-approved tirzepatide medicines exist for specific medical indications. That approval applies to the regulated prescription products and their labeled uses — it should not be interpreted as applying to unapproved research products.

**In simple terms:** two metabolic signaling pathways, one molecule, and a large body of human clinical research.
