**Trick or Truth: Do GLP-1s “eat” your muscle?**

This one gets thrown around a lot online — and it sounds scary.

**The trick:**\
“GLP-1s make you lose all your muscle.”

**The truth:**\
That’s an oversimplified claim.

When people lose a meaningful amount of weight, it’s normal for **some lean mass** to go down too. That can happen with diet, exercise, surgery, or weight-loss medication.

What matters is **what kind of tissue is being lost overall**.

Research looking at GLP1's and body composition found that **fat mass made up a major part of the weight lost**. In other words, this is not a simple “muscle wasting” story like some people make it sound.

It’s also important to remember that muscle retention depends on more than the medication alone. Things like:

* **enough protein**
* **resistance training**
* **staying active**
* **not under-eating**

can make a big difference.

**Bottom line:**\
The idea that GLP-1s automatically “eat your muscle” is more **trick** than **truth**.

Some lean mass loss can happen during weight loss, but the bigger picture from the research is that **fat loss is doing a lot of the heavy lifting**.
