⚡🧬 **WHAT IS SS-31?**

SS-31 is also known as **elamipretide**.

It is a mitochondria-targeted peptide studied for its interaction with **cardiolipin**, an important lipid in the inner mitochondrial membrane.

Because that membrane is central to mitochondrial energy production, researchers have investigated elamipretide across a range of mitochondrial and cellular-stress models.

There is also an important regulatory distinction: an FDA-approved elamipretide medicine, **Forzinity**, received accelerated approval in 2025 for a specific indication in Barth syndrome patients meeting the labeled criteria.

That approval is specific to the regulated medicine and indication and does not establish that unapproved research products are equivalent.

**In simple terms:** SS-31 research focuses directly on the machinery of the mitochondrial power plant.
