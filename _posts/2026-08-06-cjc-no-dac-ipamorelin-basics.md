🧬 **CJC NO DAC + IPAMORELIN: THE BASICS**

These two research peptides are interesting because they approach growth-hormone signaling through **different receptor systems**.

**CJC-1295 without DAC** is a GHRH-related peptide. Research focuses on signaling through the GHRH receptor.

**Ipamorelin** is a growth-hormone secretagogue studied through the **ghrelin/GHSR pathway**.

Putting the concepts side by side gives researchers a model for studying two inputs into pulsatile growth-hormone biology.

That does **not** mean a combination has established clinical benefits in humans. Research on a pathway and proof of a therapeutic effect are two very different things.

Stay curious, research friends. 🧬
