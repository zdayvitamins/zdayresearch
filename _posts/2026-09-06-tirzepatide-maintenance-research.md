🧬 **WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE INITIAL WEIGHT LOSS?**

SURMOUNT-MAINTAIN studied adults with obesity after an initial period on tirzepatide. Participants were then assigned to continue their maximum tolerated dose, reduce to 5 mg, or switch to placebo.

After another year, the reported proportions of previously achieved weight reduction maintained were **96.5%, 67.9%, and 42.8%**, respectively.

The interesting question here isn't “which dose should someone take?” This wasn't a personalized dosing experiment.

It's what maintenance research can teach us about what happens **after** substantial initial weight reduction.

Human research gets more useful when we look beyond the headline number. 🧬
