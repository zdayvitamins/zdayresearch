🧬 **WHAT IS MELANOTAN II?**

Melanotan II is a synthetic peptide studied through the **melanocortin receptor system**.

Melanocortin signaling is involved in several biological processes, including pigmentation. Early research into Melanotan II therefore focused heavily on melanocortin activity and tanning/pigmentation biology.

Researchers also observed effects involving sexual function during early human research, which helped generate interest in melanocortin signaling beyond pigmentation.

Melanotan II itself is **not an FDA-approved drug**.

**In simple terms:** one research molecule helped scientists explore how the melanocortin system can influence more than one biological pathway.
