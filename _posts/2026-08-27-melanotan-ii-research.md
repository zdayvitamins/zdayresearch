🧬 **THE UNEXPECTED SIDE OF MELANOCORTIN RESEARCH**

Melanotan II is a non-selective melanocortin receptor agonist, and much of its early research centered on **pigmentation**.

But researchers noticed something else.

Early human studies also reported effects involving **sexual function**, helping open another branch of melanocortin research.

That's one of the fun parts of science: sometimes researchers begin by studying one biological pathway and discover an entirely different effect along the way.

Those observations eventually helped drive additional research into melanocortin signaling and sexual response.

Stay curious, research friends. 🧬
