🧬 **WHAT IS KLOW?**

In Z Day research material, **KLOW** refers to:

**GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500 + KPV**

GHK-Cu has been studied in extracellular-matrix, collagen, skin, and tissue-remodeling biology.

BPC-157 and TB-500 have been investigated primarily in experimental tissue-response and repair-related models.

KPV is a small peptide fragment studied experimentally in inflammatory and immune-signaling pathways.

That makes KLOW a useful topic for looking at several different research pathways in one place.

But the distinction matters: **ingredient-level research does not establish the efficacy or safety of the combined blend in humans.**
