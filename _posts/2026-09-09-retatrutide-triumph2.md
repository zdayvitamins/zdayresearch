🧬 **RETATRUTIDE + TYPE 2 DIABETES RESEARCH**

TRIUMPH-2 looked specifically at adults with **obesity or overweight and type 2 diabetes**.

At 80 weeks, reported average body-weight reductions were **12.7%, 19.1%, and 20.8%** across the 4 mg, 9 mg, and 12 mg trial groups.

Average A1C reductions reached up to **1.6 percentage points**.

That population matters. Results in people with type 2 diabetes shouldn't automatically be treated as interchangeable with results from every other obesity trial.

Retatrutide remains investigational and is not FDA-approved as of this post. 🧬
