🧬 **KLOW: FOUR INGREDIENTS, DIFFERENT RESEARCH QUESTIONS**

KLOW contains **GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500, and KPV**.

Rather than claiming that the finished blend “does” one particular thing, let's look at the individual research areas:

**GHK-Cu** — collagen, extracellular-matrix, fibroblast, skin, and tissue-remodeling biology.

**BPC-157** — primarily preclinical research involving tissue repair and experimental injury models.

**TB-500 / thymosin-beta-4-related research** — cell migration, angiogenesis, cytoskeletal biology, and tissue remodeling.

**KPV** — experimental research involving inflammatory signaling and immune-related pathways.

These are ingredient-level research areas. They do **not** prove that combining the four ingredients creates the same outcomes, and they do not establish clinical benefits in humans.

Different ingredients. Different pathways. Lots of questions still being studied. 🧬
