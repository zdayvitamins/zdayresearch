🧬 **GHK-Cu HAS BEEN AROUND A LOT LONGER THAN SOCIAL MEDIA**

GHK is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide that was identified decades ago.

Researchers have investigated GHK-Cu across areas including **collagen and extracellular-matrix biology, fibroblast activity, skin biology, angiogenesis, and experimental tissue-repair mechanisms**.

That doesn't mean every proposed use has been established in large human clinical trials. A significant amount of the literature is preclinical or mechanistic.

But that's exactly what makes the history interesting: researchers have been asking questions about it for decades.

Stay curious. 🧬
