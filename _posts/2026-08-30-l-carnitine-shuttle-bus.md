🚌 **WHAT DOES L-CARNITINE ACTUALLY DO?**

Think of L-Carnitine like a **shuttle bus for fat.**

Fatty acids are a potential source of fuel, but long-chain fatty acids need help getting into the mitochondria — the little “power plants” inside our cells.

Carnitine participates in that transport process, allowing those fatty acids to enter mitochondrial energy metabolism.

In simple terms:

**It doesn't magically burn fat. It helps get the fuel to the power plant. ⚡**

That distinction matters.
