🧬 **WHAT IS “WOLVERINE”?**

In Z Day research material, **Wolverine** refers to a combination of:

**BPC-157 + TB-500**

Both have attracted attention in experimental research involving tissue response and repair-related biology.

BPC-157 has been investigated largely in preclinical injury and tissue models. TB-500 is related to thymosin-beta-4 biology, which has been studied in areas including cell migration, angiogenesis, and tissue remodeling.

A 2026 rat Achilles-tendon study also compared BPC-157, TB-500, and the combination during experimental tendon healing.

The important limitation: this remains largely **preclinical research** and does not establish injury-recovery benefits in humans.
