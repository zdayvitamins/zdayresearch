🧬 **WHAT IS IGF-1?**

You've probably seen IGF-1 mentioned anytime growth hormone comes up.

GH signaling stimulates production of **insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1)**, particularly in the liver, although other tissues can produce it too.

IGF-1 participates in growth, bone, muscle, and broader tissue biology. It also helps provide feedback within the GH signaling system.

Another useful distinction: GH itself is highly pulsatile, while IGF-1 is comparatively more stable.

One signal leads into another. 🧬
