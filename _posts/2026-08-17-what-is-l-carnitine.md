⚡ **WHAT IS L-CARNITINE?**

**L-Carnitine** is a naturally occurring compound that helps move long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, where those fats can be used in energy metabolism.

🚌 **WHAT IS THE CARNITINE SHUTTLE?**

Think of L-Carnitine like a **shuttle bus for fat**.

Long-chain fatty acids cannot simply move into the mitochondrial interior on their own. The **carnitine shuttle system** helps transport them across the mitochondrial membrane so they can enter the energy-making process.

⚡ **WHY DOES THAT MATTER?**

This is why L-Carnitine appears so often in research involving **fatty-acid metabolism and mitochondrial energy**.

But there is an important distinction:

**It doesn't magically burn fat. It helps get the fuel to the power plant.**

The actual metabolic outcome depends on the larger biological context.
