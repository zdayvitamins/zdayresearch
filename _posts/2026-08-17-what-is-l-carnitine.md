⚡ **WHAT IS L-CARNITINE?**

Think of L-Carnitine like a **shuttle bus for fat**.

Long-chain fatty acids need help getting into mitochondria, where they can be used in energy metabolism. Carnitine participates in the transport system that helps move those fatty acids across the mitochondrial membrane.

That is why L-Carnitine appears so often in research involving metabolism and mitochondrial energy.

But there is an important distinction:

**It doesn't magically burn fat. It helps get the fuel to the power plant. ⚡**

The actual metabolic outcome depends on the larger biological context.
