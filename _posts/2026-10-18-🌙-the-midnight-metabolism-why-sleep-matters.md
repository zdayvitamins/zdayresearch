🌙 **Your Body’s Night Shift**



While you sleep, your body keeps working—supporting tissue repair, immune function, and the signals that regulate hunger.



Cut that rest short, and the effects can follow you into the next day. 👻



🍬 **A Recipe for Cravings**



Too little quality sleep can increase hunger and cravings for calorie-dense foods. It can also reduce how well your body responds to insulin, making blood sugar harder to regulate.



That means sleep deserves a place in the conversation about metabolic health—not just food and exercise.



🎃 **Trick or Truth?**



**Trick:** “Sleep is just downtime.”



**Truth:** Sleep supports essential maintenance, including recovery and metabolic regulation.



Give your body’s night shift time to work. 🕯️
