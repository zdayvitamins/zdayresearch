✨🧬 **WHAT IS GLOW?**

In Z Day research material, **GLOW** refers to a blend of three research peptides:

**GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500**

🔬 **WHAT ARE THE INGREDIENTS STUDIED FOR?**

**GHK-Cu** has research involving collagen, fibroblasts, extracellular matrix, skin biology, and tissue remodeling.

**BPC-157** has primarily been studied in preclinical injury and tissue-response models.

**TB-500 / thymosin-beta-4-related research** includes areas such as cell movement, blood-vessel formation, and tissue remodeling.

⚠️ **WHAT'S THE IMPORTANT LIMITATION?**

Research on the individual ingredients does **not** prove that the combined GLOW blend has established clinical effects in humans.

**In simple terms:** GLOW brings together three compounds connected to different areas of experimental tissue research, but the blend itself should not be treated as a proven human therapy.
