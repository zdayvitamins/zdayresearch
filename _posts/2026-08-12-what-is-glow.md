✨🧬 **WHAT IS GLOW?**

In Z Day research material, **GLOW** refers to a blend of:

**GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500**

The reason this blend is interesting from a research perspective is that the individual components have been studied in different areas of tissue biology.

GHK-Cu has research involving collagen, fibroblasts, extracellular matrix, and tissue remodeling.

BPC-157 has primarily preclinical research involving injury and tissue-response models.

TB-500 / thymosin-beta-4-related research includes cell migration, angiogenesis, and tissue remodeling.

**Important:** evidence for the individual ingredients does not prove that the combined GLOW blend has established clinical effects in humans.
