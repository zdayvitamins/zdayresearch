🧬 **GLOW: WHAT ARE THE INGREDIENTS BEING STUDIED FOR?**

GLOW combines **GHK-Cu, BPC-157, and TB-500** as a research formulation.

The scientifically cleaner way to discuss it is to look at the ingredients individually:

**GHK-Cu** has been investigated in collagen, extracellular-matrix, fibroblast, skin, and tissue-remodeling research.

**BPC-157** has been investigated largely in preclinical models involving tissue repair and experimental injury models.

**TB-500 / thymosin-beta-4-related research** has examined cell migration, angiogenesis, and tissue-remodeling processes.

Those research areas are interesting, but evidence involving an individual ingredient does **not** establish that the combined GLOW formulation produces the same effects — particularly in humans.

Research product education means keeping that distinction clear. 🧬
