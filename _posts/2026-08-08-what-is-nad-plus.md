⚡ **WHAT IS NAD+?**

NAD+ stands for **nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide**.

It is a naturally occurring coenzyme found throughout the body and is essential to many cellular reactions.

One of its best-known jobs is helping transfer electrons during reactions involved in making cellular energy. NAD+ also participates in redox biology and serves as a substrate for enzymes involved in cellular signaling and maintenance.

NAD+ levels and metabolism are studied in aging, mitochondrial biology, metabolic research, and cellular stress.

**Important distinction:** research involving NAD+ biology does not mean every NAD+ formulation or delivery method produces the same result.

**In simple terms:** NAD+ is part of the cellular machinery that helps turn fuel into usable energy.
