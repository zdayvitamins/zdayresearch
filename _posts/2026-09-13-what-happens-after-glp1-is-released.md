🧬 **WHAT HAPPENS AFTER GLP-1 IS RELEASED?**

We talk a lot about what GLP-1 does — but another interesting question is:

**What happens to GLP-1 after the body releases it?**

GLP-1 is a naturally occurring signaling hormone, and its activity is tightly regulated. Researchers study not only its receptors, but also the enzymes and pathways involved in breaking down and clearing the signal.

Newer research has continued examining how enzymes beyond the best-known pathways may participate in GLP-1 degradation.

Why does that matter?

Because understanding a hormone means understanding its **whole life cycle** — production, release, receptor signaling, degradation, and clearance.

This is mechanistic research and should not be interpreted as a treatment recommendation.
